                                      Controls

                                      Controller

                                      No controller seen yet. Pair one with this device (a PS5 controller: hold Create and the PS button till its light flashes, then choose it in the device's Bluetooth settings), then press any button on it.

                                      Buttons

                                      Choose an action, then press the button for it, or hold a button to have it done when that button's held. Whatever had that button takes the action's old one. With a bar's trigger held, the Action Bar's slots are always □ △ ○ ✕, then ← and →.

                                      Chat and players

                                      Blocked players

                                      Their direct messages and party invites never reach you, and they aren't told. A block holds in the world you blocked them in. Block someone from the party menu's Players tab.

                                        Debug log

                                        The last five times the game froze, slowed, hit an error or crashed, the newest first, with what was going on then. Copy it to send to whoever's looking into it.

                                        Auto-snag

                                        Auto-snag takes only what's ticked in both lists: Legendary and Weapons alone, say, for legendary weapons and nothing else. Whatever it leaves is still there to tap.

                                        Action wheels

                                        Hold on yourself, a foe or one of your followers (or their icon), then flick to a slice. Flick down (S) for the wheel's other side. Tap a slice here to choose what's in it.

                                        Action Bar

                                        In a fight, these rise from the bottom of the screen (or stay there, with Always show). Tap one to use it: attacks on the foe you're fighting, anything else on yourself. Hold one to change it, or flick a spell up or down for the next or last spell of its school. There are two bars, Action Bar 1 and Action Bar 2: swap between them with the Swap Bar button at the bar's right end (or `). Choose a bar here, then tap a slot to choose what's in it.
