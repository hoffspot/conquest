    Tap the ground to walk, double‑tap to run, tap an enemy to fight it, or someone to talk to them. Or steer with the stick in the bottom left — push it over to run — or W, A, S, D (Shift to run). Hold on yourself or an enemy for spells, or on the minimap for the world map. Your pack and journal are top right (I and J).

        Quick actions

        In a fight, these four rise from the bottom of the screen. Tap one to use it: attacks on the foe you're fighting, anything else on yourself. Hold one to change it. Tap a slot here to choose what's in it.
