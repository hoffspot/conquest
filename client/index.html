                        Debug log

                        The last five times the game froze, slowed, hit an error or crashed, the newest first, with what was going on then. Copy it to send to whoever's looking into it.

                        Auto-snag

                        Auto-snag takes only what's ticked in both lists: Legendary and Weapons alone, say, for legendary weapons and nothing else. Whatever it leaves is still there to tap.

                        Action wheels

                        Hold on yourself or a foe, then flick to a slice. Flick down (S) for the wheel's other side. Tap a slice here to choose what's in it.

                        Quick actions

                        In a fight, these rise from the bottom of the screen. Tap one to use it: attacks on the foe you're fighting, anything else on yourself. Hold one to change it, or flick a spell up or down for the next or last spell of its school. Tap a slot here to choose what's in it.
