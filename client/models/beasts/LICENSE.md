# The beasts' models

## dreadthorn.bin

The **Dreadthorn Dragon**, by **HuyKhoi2407**, bought on **Superhive** (formerly Blender Market).

It is not CC0 and not free to reuse. It's in the game with permission, which this repository's
owner obtained, and only in this converted form: the bought model put through
`utilities/blenderpipeline` (`private/dreadthorn-phone.json`, which stays out of the repository
with the bought files). That build made the 34,266-triangle mesh with two lower details packed in,
1,024-pixel KTX2 textures, and six clips made up from its own (a bite, a claw swipe, a tail sweep,
a breath of fire on the ground and hovering, and a death).

It's scrambled as the game serves it, with whose it is in its glTF asset's `copyright`. It isn't
in this repository, but in the private one the game's builds fetch it from
(`docs/LICENSED_MODELS.md`).

Don't copy it out of this game or use it in anything else.

## werewolf-2.bin, and its skins: werewolf.dusk.bin, .ash, .russet, .moonfang, .rotten

**Werewolf - Mythical Creature**, bought on **Fab**
(<https://www.fab.com/listings/cb347314-44c4-416e-9367-893073f931d6>) by this repository's owner
under **Fab's Standard License**.

It is not CC0 and not free to reuse. That licence lets it be used in a game its buyer makes and
distributed as part of it, never on its own, so it's in the game only in this converted form,
served scrambled as part of the game: the bought FBX put through `utilities/blenderpipeline`
(`private/werewolf.json`, which stays out of the repository with the bought files), its `browser`
build. That's 16,456 triangles with two lower details packed in, 1,024-pixel KTX2 textures and
nine clips: its own six, and three made up from them (a howl, a claw swipe and a run). Each skin holds only the textures that differ, made from the asset's own for its Skin2
to Skin6.

They're scrambled as the game serves them, with whose they are in each glTF asset's `copyright`.
They aren't in this repository, but in the private one the game's builds fetch them from
(`docs/LICENSED_MODELS.md`). The model viewer shows the werewolf (*Coming to the wilds*) until it's
in the game.

Don't copy it out of this game or use it in anything else.
