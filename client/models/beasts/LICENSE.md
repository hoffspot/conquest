# The beasts' models

## dreadthorn.bin

The **Dreadthorn Dragon**, by **HuyKhoi2407**, bought on **Superhive** (formerly Blender Market).

It is not CC0 and not free to reuse. It's in the game with permission, which this repository's
owner obtained, and only in this converted form: the bought model put through
`utilities/blenderpipeline` (`private/dreadthorn-phone.json`, which stays out of the repository
with the bought files). That build made the 34,266-triangle mesh with two lower details packed in,
1,024-pixel KTX2 textures, and six clips made up from its own (a bite, a claw swipe, a tail sweep,
a breath of fire on the ground and hovering, and a death).

It's scrambled as the game serves it, with whose it is in its glTF asset's `copyright`. It isn't
in this repository, but in the private one the game's builds fetch it from
(`docs/LICENSED_MODELS.md`).

Don't copy it out of this game or use it in anything else.
